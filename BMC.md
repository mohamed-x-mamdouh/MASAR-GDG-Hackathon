### Customer Segments
- University students seeking career guidance and internship readiness.
- Students willing to pay for personalized learning plans and mentorship.
- Training companies seeking qualified student candidates.
- Future customers: universities and educational institutions.

### Value Propositions
- Personalized career roadmaps based on structured skill maps and industry requirements.
- Clear identification and prioritization of skill gaps.
- Curated learning resources and practical projects.
- Human mentorship and project feedback.
- Evidence-based skill portfolios that demonstrate actual student progress.
- For training companies: better visibility into candidates' demonstrated skills and project experience.

### Channels
- Masar website.
- University student communities and clubs.
- Student ambassadors.
- Social media and educational content.
- University career events and workshops.
- Direct partnerships with training companies.

### Customer Relationships
- Personalized learning journeys.
- Automated progress tracking and reminders.
- Paid mentorship packages and individual sessions.
- Weekly mentor follow-ups.
- Project reviews and continuous feedback.
- Student community and peer support.

### Revenue Streams
- Student monthly subscriptions.
- Premium learning and mentorship packages.
- Individual paid mentoring sessions.
- Training company subscriptions or paid recruitment campaigns.
- Sponsored practical challenges and training programs.
- Future institutional partnerships with universities.

### Key Resources
- Web platform and AI capabilities.
- Career skill maps and industry requirements data.
- Curated learning resources and practical projects.
- Qualified mentors and their expertise.
- Student assessment and project evidence.
- Technical team and operational processes.

- Developing and maintaining career skill maps.
- Assessing student skills and identifying gaps.
- Personalizing learning plans using approved resources.
- Managing mentors, sessions, and project reviews.
- Tracking student progress and validating demonstrated skills.
- Building partnerships with training companies.
- Improving recommendations using student outcomes and mentor feedback.
